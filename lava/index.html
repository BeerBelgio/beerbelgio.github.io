FULL LAVA

MOVE
Move the pointer through the field to create a local fluid perturbation.
SCROLL
Scroll to push the whole lava field and disturb the blobs.
GRAB + DRAG
Select a blob, move it, then release it. The throw keeps part of your gesture before returning to autonomous motion.
HOLD
Keep a blob selected and still: its liquid warping increases while you hold it, then fades after release.
5 + 5 CLICKS
Five clicks on empty space repel the blobs. The next five attract them. Then the cycle starts again.
